## Description

```
Ledgerline is a pay record that a migrant domestic worker keeps herself. Each month she writes down what she expected and what she received. Her employer can acknowledge or disagree from a link. When pay goes missing, she has a record she made at the time that no one else can change, and she can share only the months she chooses.

This demo runs on the live app against the Base Sepolia test network. The sample worker, the employer and the bank data are all demo data. No money moves.

Try it (test network): https://ledgerline-sg.vercel.app
Source code: https://github.com/successaje/ledgerline
Hackathon build: https://dorahacks.io/build/50263

How it works
Records are attestations on Ethereum Attestation Service, signed by a key kept on the worker's phone. Names, notes and dates stay on the phone; months, amounts and a hash go to the public test network. The employer's response is a separate attestation signed from their own phone. A shared link lets anyone re-check the chosen months against the public record. Bank evidence in this version uses demo data and says so.

What this is not
Not a payslip from an employer, not legal advice, not connected to a real bank, and not a way to send or receive money.

Built for the Web3 for Social Good 2026 hackathon (SMU, GSR Foundation, University of Malaga). Tech Track entry.
```
