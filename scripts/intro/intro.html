For migrant domestic workers in Singapore.

When pay goes missing, the only record is the employer's word.

Screenshots and chat messages are easy to dispute. Nothing was written down at the time, by the worker, that anyone else can check.

Each month, write down what you expected and what you received.

Signed by a key on your phone. Names and notes stay on the phone; the amounts go to a public test network and cannot be changed later.
